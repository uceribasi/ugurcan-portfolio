PasteCompiler

A desktop app that detects clipboard data, converts it into 17 output formats, reports changes and saves reusable recipes.

RustTauriReactTypeScript
Visit website ↗GitHub ↗
Technical notes+
  • Data processing across JSON, CSV/TSV, YAML, TOML, XML, SQL and other formats.
  • Round-trip checking for supported outputs; SQL, templates and plain text do not support that verification.
  • Reusable, shareable conversion recipes, with offline rules or optional AI providers.

02 / MY APPROACH

I see the interface.
I think in systems.

I have been developing software since 2014. I work across frontend and Backend-for-Frontend layers with React, Next.js, TypeScript and NestJS, with a focus on micro frontends, GraphQL and modular system design.

Technical governance, architectural decisions and team development were an important part of my experience as a team lead. Today I combine that background with LLM applications and data-focused personal projects.

I see building as a way of learning. Readling, NU AI Video Editor and PasteCompiler are my open-source products for language learning, video editing and data conversion.
