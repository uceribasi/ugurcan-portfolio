03 / SELECTED PROJECTS

Products that started
with my own questions.

Three projects I took from an idea to a working product, with their source code shared openly.

Web · Language learning

Readling

An open-source language learning app that brings reading, vocabulary and spaced repetition into one flow.

ReactTypeScriptIndexedDBFSRSBrowser AI
Visit website ↗GitHub ↗
Technical notes+
  • Word and phrase tracking in text, EPUB and PDF, with AI explanations in sentence context.
  • Browser-stored library and vocabulary history, with FSRS-based review and tests.
  • On-device AI or a provider chosen by the user; core reading and practice without an account.

Desktop · Video & AI

NU AI Video Editor

A Mac app that removes pauses, retakes and filler sounds from recordings, with subtitles and beta dubbing.

RustTauriReactwhisper.cppffmpeg
Visit website ↗GitHub ↗
Technical notes+
  • Local speech analysis, word-timed transcripts and cut suggestions the user can review.
  • Captions that follow edits, with SRT/VTT, selectable language tracks or burned-in output.
  • Optional AI review and translation, with local voice cloning and dubbing in beta for authorized voices.

Desktop · Developer tool

PasteCompiler

A desktop app that detects clipboard data, converts it into 17 output formats, reports changes and saves reusable recipes.

RustTauriReactTypeScript
Visit website ↗GitHub ↗
Technical notes+
  • Data processing across JSON, CSV/TSV, YAML, TOML, XML, SQL and other formats.
  • Round-trip checking for supported outputs; SQL, templates and plain text do not support that verification.
  • Reusable, shareable conversion recipes, with offline rules or optional AI providers.
